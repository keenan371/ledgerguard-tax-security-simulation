# LedgerGuard Executive Summary — Fictional Simulation

The simulation demonstrates a repeatable process: assess, prioritize, remediate agreed items, document, verify, preserve evidence, and review with the owner. Three priorities were MFA/access governance, restore-test evidence, and incident-readiness documentation. This is a fictional training deliverable and must never be presented as a client outcome.

## 30/60/90 roadmap
- **30 days:** confirm identity/role inventory and MFA evidence.
- **60 days:** run restore-test and access-review records.
- **90 days:** tabletop the incident procedure and refresh evidence register.
