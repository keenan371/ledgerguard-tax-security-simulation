# WISP and Procedures — Fictional Training Draft

**NOT LEGAL ADVICE. NOT A CLIENT POLICY. HUMAN/LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.**

The fictional organization assigns the owner/CPA accountability for information safeguards, maintains an asset and role inventory, enforces MFA, reviews access quarterly and at termination, protects endpoints, verifies backups, limits data sharing to approved vendors, and records incidents/evidence. Detailed procedures define account lifecycle, acceptable use, access control, backup review, and incident escalation.
