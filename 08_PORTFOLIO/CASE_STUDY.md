# Portfolio Case Study — Fictional Simulation

**LedgerGuard Tax & Accounting, LLC is a fictional training environment.**

## Scenario
A seven-person simulated Central Florida accounting firm needed a structured way to connect written safeguards to practical identity, endpoint, backup, and incident-readiness evidence.

## Work performed
Created a bounded intake, baseline evidence index, fictional risk register, safe control simulations, verification checklist, owner-friendly roadmap, and time/cost model.

## What this proves
It proves the structure and documentation of a simulated process, not a real client engagement, certification, security outcome, or production control test.
